Who it is for

Rehab that starts before your operation

Many people only see a physio after surgery. Starting beforehand means you go into your operation stronger, you know your first exercises already, and the early weeks feel less daunting.

After surgery, rehab is built in clear stages. Your physio works to your surgeon's protocol, checks your progress at each step and moves you on when you are ready, not on a fixed timetable.

This may suit you if

  • You have surgery booked and want to prepare
  • You have had surgery and are ready to start rehab
  • Your surgeon has given you a rehab protocol to follow
  • Your recovery has stalled and you want a clear plan
  • You want to return to sport, work or the things you enjoy

What to expect

Your surgery rehab journey

  1. Before surgeryAn assessment, then strength and movement work so you go into your operation as strong as possible and know your first exercises.
  2. Early recoverySettling pain and swelling, getting moving safely and protecting the repair, following your surgeon's protocol.
  3. Back to full strengthProgressive strength work in our rehab gym, with clear checkpoints before you return to sport, work or running.

Your first appointment

What happens

We talk through your surgery, your goals and any protocol from your surgeon, then assess how you are moving.

How long

Your first appointment is longer than a follow-up. You will see the exact length when you book.

What to bring

Your surgeon's rehab protocol or discharge letter, any scan reports, and comfortable clothes.

When to book

Before surgery, a few weeks ahead if you can. After surgery, as soon as your surgeon says rehab can start.

Funding and FAQs

Funding and common questions

Funding and referrals

  • No referral needed. Book a private physio appointment directly.
  • Private health insurance. Physiotherapy may be claimable on private health extras, depending on your fund and level of cover.
  • Medicare, DVA, workers compensation or CTP? Please call or email before booking so we can check the paperwork you need.

Questions before you book

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book a private physio appointment directly. Bring any protocol or letter from your surgeon to your first appointment.

When should I start before surgery?

A few weeks before your operation is ideal, but even one or two appointments help you prepare.

When can I start after surgery?

When your surgeon says rehab can begin. This is often within the first week or two, but it depends on your operation.

Will you follow my surgeon's protocol?

Yes. Your rehab follows your surgeon's protocol, and we can keep your surgeon updated on your progress if you would like.

How long does rehab take?

It depends on the surgery and your goals. Some people need a few weeks, others several months, for example after an ACL reconstruction. Your physio will give you a clear idea at your first appointment.

Can I claim on private health insurance?

Physiotherapy may be claimable on private health extras, depending on your fund and level of cover.
